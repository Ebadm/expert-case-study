**Client:** fictional strategy consultancy advising an infrastructure investor

**Project type:** strategy

**Timeline:** 1-2 weeks for initial outreach

### Objective

Assess the economics of private rail freight operation in Poland for 2025-2030: the shift from coal volumes to intermodal, infrastructure access charges and how they are set, train path availability on the congested corridors, locomotive fleet and traction economics, the driver shortage, and where rail can realistically take share from road.

### Independent industry experts

We want independent operators and planners, not vendors selling rolling stock or software:

- former network planning, train path allocation or access charge leads at the Polish infrastructure manager or the rail regulator;
- former operations, commercial or fleet leads at private freight operators active in Poland;
- terminal and intermodal operators who handle the transfer between rail and road.

Ideally 8+ years in the Polish rail sector, with hands-on involvement in at least one new operating licence, terminal or corridor service that reached commercial operation. Polish language experience is expected; the interview can be in English.

**Screening questions:**

- Which Polish rail freight operations have you been directly involved in, and in what role?
- How do you expect access charges and train path availability to develop over the next five years?
- What are realistic lead times today for launching a new intermodal service, and what drives them?
- What would make you walk away from a Polish rail freight investment?

### Excluded companies

- Do not include current employees of PKP Cargo, PKP PLK, DB Cargo Polska or UTK (the regulator).
