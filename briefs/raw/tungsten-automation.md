**Client:** fictional global private-equity firm

**Project type:** commercial and technical due diligence

**Target:** Tungsten Automation — intelligent document processing, capture and workflow automation. The company traded as Kofax until 2024, when it was renamed Tungsten Automation under private-equity ownership. Most public profiles and press coverage still say Kofax.

**Timeline:** 1-2 weeks for initial outreach

### Objective

Evaluate the target's product competitiveness, customer retention and strategic outlook after the rebrand, in a market where LLM-based extraction has reset what buyers expect from document capture. We want to speak with three groups.

### 1. Former employees

Left in the last 3 years. Internal view of product strategy and roadmap execution; how the business responded to LLM-based extraction entering the category; sales-cycle complexity and competitive pressure; product gaps customers flagged repeatedly; retention and culture through the rebrand.

**Ideal titles:** former Product Managers, Engineers, Solutions Architects, Sales Executives and GTM leaders; ex VP or Director in Product, Engineering, Sales or Partnerships.

**Screening questions:**

- What was your role, and which teams or customer accounts did you work with?
- What were the biggest product or commercial challenges during your time there?
- How did the company position itself as LLM-based extraction entered the category?
- Which product gaps or weaknesses did customers routinely flag?

### 2. Customers (current or former)

Direct experience implementing or running the product in an enterprise, at meaningful volume. We want to compare accuracy in production, total cost of ownership, support quality and switching behaviour.

**Ideal titles:** Heads of Automation, Document Processing or Shared Services; IT Directors; Automation CoE leads; decision-makers in enterprise software selection.

**Screening questions:**

- Are you a current or former customer, and over what period?
- What influenced your selection over the alternatives you evaluated?
- How would you rate accuracy in production against what was promised in the pilot?
- Have you considered switching vendors, and what would trigger it?

### 3. Competitors

Current or former (up to 2 years out) product, go-to-market or partnerships leaders at ABBYY, Hyland, OpenText, IBM or Microsoft who can discuss competitive positioning and win/loss against the target. No founders or C-level.

### Excluded companies

- Do not include current employees of Tungsten Automation. Note that the same company appears as Kofax on most profiles, and both names are excluded.
- Former employees must have left at least 6 months ago.
